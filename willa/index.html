Meet Willa

Air traffic controller for working parents, helping you get your kids where they need to be.

How it works

  1. Willa spots a conflict It's 2PM. Thursday's practice moved to 4:00PM. You were supposed to handle pickup/dropoff for practice, but now you've got a major conflict.
  2. Willa alerts you with a proposal Willa sees the conflict, checks your calendars, your partner's, and your village's calendars. She finds your best option and makes a proposal to you.
  3. You decide Reply yes or no, and Willa will do the coordinating for you.
  4. Willa updates your calendars Confirm again and Willa updates your calendars.
  5. Day-of reminders Whoever's covering gets the details they need, all without you sending additional texts.

Rebuilding the village

We've forgotten what "it takes a village" really means.

For most of human history, raising kids was shared work. The nuclear family — two parents managing alone — is barely eighty years old. In 1950, 21% of Americans lived in a multigenerational household. By 1980, 12%.

But the village didn't move away. The median American still lives eighteen miles from their mother. It's just that everyone works now, nobody holds the whole schedule, and eighteen miles at three o'clock might as well be three states.

What feels like a time problem is really a coordination problem. That's what Willa is for.

What you can do with Willa

  • Assign pickup or dropoff to someone in your village
  • Get a Sunday email to plan the week ahead
  • Add a nanny or babysitter to your village
  • Add grandparents or another family to your village
  • Spot calendar conflicts before they become a problem

Frequently asked questions

How are you approaching privacy?

Privacy's our First Officer. We are selective about who we work with (human-wise and tech-wise). We are always thinking about ways to improve the state of privacy and security, and where that fits in the roadmap.

Can my village members see my calendar?

No, they cannot by design. We've also implemented safeguards and designed Willa to prevent accidental cross-leaks too.

Will Willa ever change my calendar without asking?

No. Nothing is written to your calendar until you reply yes.

Who is Willa for?

Working parents with recurring weekly schedule changes, who rely on grandparents and/or friends for carpooling.

How do you maintain quality?

We currently use human curation to evaluate every confirmation, notification and proposal that goes out to you.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Willa's SMS-only by design. Willa should fit into your routine, and not the other way around.

Which calendars does Willa work with?

Google Calendar and Apple/iCloud.

Can I connect my work calendar?

Yes, if your company permits it. Willa works best when she can see work calendars.

What if Willa gets it wrong?

If something still falls through our safeguards and measures, reach out to us directly. You'll receive the contact information after signing up.

Are you SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified?

Not currently, but we plan to down the road.
