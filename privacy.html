AY Consulting dba Sunny Launch ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. By using our Services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number, and names of other individuals relevant to the Services
  • Profile information: Schedules, preferences, logistical details, and other context you choose to share
  • Communications: Messages you send and receive through our Services, including SMS replies and email correspondence

Information from Third-Party Services

  • Google Calendar data: When you connect your Google Calendar account, we access your calendar events (including event titles, times, locations, and attendee information) to detect changes and, with your explicit confirmation, write entries to your calendar. We request read and write access to your calendar events. We do not access other Google account data beyond calendar events.
  • Apple Calendar (iCloud) data: When you connect an Apple Calendar via app-specific password, we access events (titles, times, locations, descriptions) from the calendar you select. Read-only — we cannot modify or delete events on Apple-side. We do not access other Apple data (mail, contacts, photos, reminders).
  • SMS delivery data: When we send or receive SMS messages through our messaging provider, metadata such as delivery status, timestamps, and phone numbers is processed.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: How you interact with our Services, including feature usage and service activity timestamps

We use the information we collect to:

  • Train and improve our systems: Your interactions with recommendations (confirmations, rejections, and modifications) may be used to improve the accuracy and quality of future suggestions

3. AI and Automated Processing

Our Services use artificial intelligence (AI) to analyze calendar data, detect conflicts, and generate recommendations. Specifically:

  • Calendar analysis: AI processes your calendar events to identify changes, conflicts, and opportunities

AI-generated recommendations are suggestions only. You always retain the ability to accept, reject, or modify any suggestion before it affects your calendar.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Apple Calendar (iCloud) — App-Specific Password Handling

When you connect your Apple Calendar, you generate an app-specific password at appleid.apple.com and paste it into our setup flow. Specifics:

  • The password lets us read events from one Apple calendar you select. It cannot read your email, contacts, photos, reminders, or any other Apple data.
  • The password cannot change or delete events on your calendar — read only.
  • We store it encrypted at rest. The encryption key sits in our hosting environment, separate from the database.
  • You can revoke the password at any time at appleid.apple.com → Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords → Willa. This immediately stops our access; we will detect the revocation on the next sync attempt and stop trying.
  • If you change your main Apple ID password, all app-specific passwords (including ours) are revoked automatically by Apple. We will text you to ask for a new one.
  • We do not have your main Apple ID password and do not want it.
